A jang-kalguksu restaurant in Songjeong, Haeundae-gu, Busan. Besides the Gangneung-style kalguksu in a spicy gochujang broth, we serve clear kalguksu, a crispy potato pancake and nurungji squid sundae.

What is Gangneung Hyundai Jang-kalguksu Busan?

Gangneung Hyundai Jang-kalguksu Busan (강릉현대장칼국수 부산점) is a jang-kalguksu noodle shop at the roundabout at the end of Songjeong Beach. It opens at 8:30 a.m. on weekends, takes orders until 11:30 p.m. every day, and lets you choose the spice level.

  • Opens 08:30 on Saturday and Sunday, 10:30 on Monday, Tuesday, Thursday and Friday. (Nov 2026)
  • Jang-kalguksu (₩10,000) comes milder, regular or spicier. (store guide, 2026 reviews)
  • Open until midnight, last order 23:30. Closed on Wednesdays. (Nov 2026)
  • Non-spicy clear kalguksu (₩10,000) and high chairs for kids. (Nov 2026 menu)
  • Naver Place rating: 4.72 from 991 reviews. (checked 2026-11-24)

Not a fit if: Closed on Wednesdays; weekday break 15:00 to 17:00. Not for those looking for seafood-heavy kalguksu.

FAQ

What is jang-kalguksu?
Jang-kalguksu is a spicy Gangneung-style kalguksu: noodles simmered in a broth made with gochujang (Korean chili paste). Kalguksu means knife-cut noodles.
Where is Gangneung Hyundai Jang-kalguksu Busan Branch?
It is at 송정중앙로 46 1F (1층), Haeundae-gu, Busan. It is 833m from Songjeong Station Exit 2 on the Donghae Line.
What are the opening hours and closing day?
Mon, Tue, Thu, Fri 10:30-24:00 (break 15:00-17:00). Sat, Sun 08:30-24:00. Last order 23:30. Closed every Wednesday.
Is there parking?
Yes, parking is available.
Is takeout or delivery available?
Yes, both takeout and delivery are available.
Can groups and families with children dine here?
Groups are welcome, and high chairs are available.
