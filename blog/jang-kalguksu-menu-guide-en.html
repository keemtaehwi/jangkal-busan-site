What Is Jang-kalguksu? What to Order at a Busan Kalguksu Restaurant

Written by the restaurant on 24 Nov 2026. Customer quotes are translated from Naver Place reviews.

Quick answer

Jang-kalguksu (장칼국수) is a Gangneung-style knife-cut noodle soup in a gochujang-based broth. Gangneung Hyundai Jang-kalguksu Busan Branch (강릉현대장칼국수 부산점) serves it in Songjeong, Haeundae-gu, Busan, and you can choose a milder or spicier bowl.

What to order

Start with jang-kalguksu. For something without chili, ask for clear kalguksu. Show the Korean name to the staff or pick it on the kiosk.

Choosing your spice level

  • Jang-kalguksu can be made less spicy or spicier.
  • Jang-gukbap with meat (고기장국밥) is on the spicy side.
  • Clear kalguksu (맑은칼국수) is not spicy.

One reviewer wrote that choosing less spicy was a good call, with a deep and rich flavor (translated Naver Place review, 2026.07.31).
