Where to eat after visiting Haedong Yonggungsa Temple: a noodle stop in Songjeong

Written November 27, 2026 by the restaurant. Prices are in-store (dine-in) prices.

In short

Haedong Yonggungsa is about 2.1 km from Songjeong Beach by the coastal signposts: roughly 10 minutes by car or 30–35 minutes on foot. At the end of Songjeong Beach, Gangneung Hyundai Jang-Kalguksu serves spicy jang-kalguksu (₩10,000) and a non-spicy clear kalguksu (₩10,000).

Why it works after the temple

  • A hot noodle soup is welcome after the windy seaside temple.
  • On weekends it opens at 08:30, so early temple visitors can eat right after.
  • There is a non-spicy option for children and older travelers.

Not the right fit if…

  • you want to eat within walking distance of the temple gate
  • you are looking for grilled eel or other Gijang seafood specialties
  • you are visiting on a Wednesday (closed)

Menu highlights

  • Jang-kalguksu ₩10,000
  • Clear kalguksu (not spicy) ₩10,000
  • Crispy potato pancake ₩12,000
  • Dumplings ₩8,000
