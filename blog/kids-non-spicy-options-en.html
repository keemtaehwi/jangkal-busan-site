Eating With Kids at a Busan Kalguksu Restaurant: Non-Spicy Options

Written by the restaurant on 24 Nov 2026. Customer quotes are translated from Naver Place reviews.

Quick answer

Gangneung Hyundai Jang-kalguksu Busan Branch (강릉현대장칼국수 부산점) in Songjeong, Haeundae-gu, Busan has a non-spicy clear kalguksu (맑은칼국수), high chairs and dumplings, so families with young children can eat comfortably.

Our menu says kids really like the clear kalguksu, eaten by rolling noodles with seasoned radish. Jang-kalguksu can also be ordered less spicy.

Facilities for families

  • High chairs are available.
  • Seats are a mix of tables for two and four.
  • Takeout and delivery are available.

Reviews from guests (translated Naver Place reviews): "High chairs are provided, so it is easy to visit with a child" (2026.02.13); "Really delicious. I came with my kids and they ate happily" (2026.10.18).

FAQ

Is there a non-spicy dish for children?
Yes, clear kalguksu (맑은칼국수, ₩10,000) is not spicy.
Are high chairs available?
Yes.
Are there dumplings?
Yes: meat (희야만두), kimchi (빼롱만두) and half and half (풍자만두), each ₩8,000.
