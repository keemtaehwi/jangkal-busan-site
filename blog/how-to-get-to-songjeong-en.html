How to Get to Gangneung Hyundai Jang-kalguksu Busan (Songjeong Station)

Written by the restaurant on 24 Nov 2026. Customer quotes are translated from Naver Place reviews.

Quick answer

Gangneung Hyundai Jang-kalguksu Busan Branch (강릉현대장칼국수 부산점) is in Songjeong, Haeundae-gu, Busan. It is 833m from Songjeong Station Exit 2 on the Donghae Line, at the roundabout at the end of Songjeong Beach. Parking is available.

Getting here

  • By train: take the Donghae Line to Songjeong Station (송정역) and use Exit 2. The restaurant is 833m away.
  • Landmark: the roundabout at the end of Songjeong Beach.
  • By car: parking is available.

You can paste this address into a map app: 부산광역시 해운대구 송정중앙로 46 1층.

How to order at the kiosk

Ordering is done at the kiosk near the entrance. Pick your dishes (the Korean names are 장칼국수, 맑은칼국수, 빠작감자전 and so on), then pay. A reviewer noted that seats are a mix of tables for two and for four (translated Naver Place review, 2026.05.23). Spice level for jang-kalguksu can be set to less or more spicy.

Address, phone and prices

  • Address: 부산광역시 해운대구 송정중앙로 46 1층 (Songjeong, Haeundae-gu, Busan)
