Where to eat lunch near Songjeong Beach, Busan: spicy Gangneung-style jang-kalguksu

Written November 27, 2026 by the restaurant. Prices are in-store (dine-in) prices.

In short

If you want a hot, hearty Korean lunch near Songjeong Beach, Gangneung Hyundai Jang-Kalguksu (Busan branch) sits at the roundabout at the end of the beach. Jang-kalguksu is knife-cut noodle soup in a spicy gochujang–doenjang broth, a specialty of Gangneung on Korea's east coast. A bowl is 10,000 won.

What to order

  • Jang-kalguksu (spicy noodle soup) ₩10,000
  • Clear kalguksu (not spicy, good for kids) ₩10,000
  • Gogi jang-gukbap (spicy beef rice soup) ₩13,000
  • Crispy potato pancake (bbajak gamjajeon) ₩12,000
  • Squid sundae with crispy rice ₩18,000
  • Dumplings (meat, kimchi, half-half) ₩8,000 each

Good to know

  • Ordering is done at a kiosk; a Korean–English menu guide is on this site.
  • Weekends open at 08:30, so it also works for a late breakfast after a sunrise walk.
  • Closed every Wednesday. Weekday break time is 15:00–17:00.

Not the right fit if…

  • you want a sea-view table
  • you are looking for raw fish or seafood set meals
  • you cannot eat spicy food at all and dislike plain broth

Getting there

Walk along Songjeong Beach to the roundabout at its end. From Songjeong Station (Donghae Line) Exit 2 it is 833 m. Haedong Yonggungsa Temple and the Osiria tourist complex (Lotte Premium Outlets, Lotte World Busan, IKEA) are about 10 minutes away by car.
